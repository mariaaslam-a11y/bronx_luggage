Our story

Great luggage is passed down, not thrown away.

There’s a special feeling in receiving a truly high-quality piece of luggage. The best sets are handed down by family members who have travelled the world, carrying their stories from one generation to the next.

Bronx Luggage, located in the Festival Marketplace, offers a wide selection — from affordable sets to the finest brands on the market. Alongside suitcases we stock backpacks, travel accessories, briefcases and much more, all chosen to make every journey a little more remarkable.
